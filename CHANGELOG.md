# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- `net.gaiadesk:embed-android`: share your app's own window with your support team from a "Get
  help" button — consent enforced by the library, an always-visible "Sharing with … · Stop"
  indicator in your activity (frames only while it is on screen), masked views painted out before
  encoding, view-only by default with an opt-in guided mode dispatched to your own views, and an
  opt-in whole-screen share through MediaProjection.
- Views and Compose examples.
